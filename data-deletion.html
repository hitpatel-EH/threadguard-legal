ThreadGuard AI

Data Deletion Instructions

1. About Data Deletion

ThreadGuard AI respects users' requests to delete applicable information processed or stored by the application.

These instructions explain how to request deletion of applicable ThreadGuard AI data.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of applicable ThreadGuard AI data, contact the ThreadGuard AI project administrator using the contact information provided by the application owner.

Please include enough information to identify the relevant account or data associated with the deletion request.

Important: Do not include passwords, access tokens, API secrets, or other sensitive authentication credentials in a deletion request.

3. What May Be Deleted

Depending on the information involved, a deletion request may apply to information stored by ThreadGuard AI such as:

  • Threads-related information stored by ThreadGuard AI
  • ThreadGuard AI analysis records
  • Monitoring records associated with stored Threads posts
  • Other applicable information maintained by ThreadGuard AI

4. Deletion Requests and Verification

ThreadGuard AI may need to verify a deletion request before processing it in order to protect users and prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Verification information will be used only as necessary to process the request and protect the relevant data.

5. Processing of Requests

After receiving a valid deletion request, ThreadGuard AI will review the request and process deletion of applicable information maintained by the application, subject to any applicable legal, security, or operational requirements.

6. Threads and Meta Data

ThreadGuard AI uses the official Threads API provided by Meta. Information held by Meta or the Threads service is subject to Meta's own policies and procedures.

This page describes deletion of information maintained by ThreadGuard AI and does not represent a deletion request system for Meta's own systems.
